# 可樂噴多遠音效

遊戲使用下列實錄素材的片段，並做裁切、等化、增益與淡出。

- `slosh-soft-*.mp3` and `slosh-strong-*.mp3` — 開蓋前的搖瓶片段，取自 [screw_cap_cola_shake_fizz_carbonated_2](https://freesound.org/people/Geoff-Bremner-Audio/sounds/751056/) by [Geoff Bremner](https://linktr.ee/gbaudio), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- `thread.mp3` — [Soda Bottle Opening (Plastic, Carbonation Release)](https://freesound.org/people/Flip_Book/sounds/850379/) by Flip_Book, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- `hiss.mp3` and `burst.mp3` — [Soda Water Bottle.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Greencouch/sounds/106264/) by Greencouch, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- `spray.mp3` and `bubbles.mp3` — [Fizzy Drink Pouring Into Glass](https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/700351/) by Rudmer_Rotteveel, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- `splash.mp3` — [splat.wav](https://freesound.org/people/zolopher/sounds/44203/) by zolopher, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

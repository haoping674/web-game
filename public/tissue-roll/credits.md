# Boxed tissue artwork and audio

The recordings are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses excerpts and mixes them at runtime.

- slide.mp3 — [Tissue Pull](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/320153/) by OwlStorm (Ashe Kirk)
- draw.mp3 — [Tissue pulled out of tissue box.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Lian3/sounds/595624/) by Lian3
- fibers.mp3 — [soft-tear.wav](https://freesound.org/people/birdOfTheNorth/sounds/613295/) by birdOfTheNorth
- handling.mp3 — [paper - crumple 02.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Anthousai/sounds/398897/) by Anthousai
- paper-desk.mp3 — [dropping and sliding paper.wav](https://freesound.org/people/123jorre456/sounds/46625/) by 123jorre456
- box-drop.mp3 — [foley_Cardboard Box Drop.wav](https://freesound.org/people/ittou/sounds/465451/) by ittou
- box-tap.mp3 — [hit-tap-strike-cardboard-box-13-times.wav](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/364932/) by newagesoup

The soft wrappers, boxes and printable textures are drawn by the game using visual references for May Flower, Kleenex, and Kirkland Signature. The game is an independent recreation. Product photographs are not embedded.

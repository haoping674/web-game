# Boxed tissue artwork and audio

The three recordings are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses excerpts and mixes them at runtime.

- slide.mp3 — [Tissue Pull](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/320153/) by OwlStorm (Ashe Kirk)
- draw.mp3 — [Tissue pulled out of tissue box.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Lian3/sounds/595624/) by Lian3
- fibers.mp3 — [soft-tear.wav](https://freesound.org/people/birdOfTheNorth/sounds/613295/) by birdOfTheNorth

The box, botanical art and printable textures are drawn by the game. [May Flower](https://www.yfycpg.com/zh-TW/products/1) and 五月花 identify the reference product. The game is an independent recreation. It does not embed product photographs or the manufacturer's logo image.
